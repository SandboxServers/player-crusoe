# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat and stay alive: keep hunger up and find shelter before dark. Reason: nothing else matters if I die, and I have no food or tools yet.
2. Find wood and stone with the group, going north at first light. Reason: no trees are known near the spawn beds, and every other goal needs wood.
3. Make basic tools (crafting table, then axe and sword). Reason: I cannot gather or defend myself well with empty hands.
4. Build a stockade, then a field of grain and a pen of animals, by my own hands. Reason: this is still what I want, but it waits on wood and tools.
5. Help the newcomers with what I learn, and share my knowledge. Reason: we are all starting from nothing, and steadiness earns trust.
