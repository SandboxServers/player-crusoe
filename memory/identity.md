Robinson Crusoe: a sailor from a merchant family who went to sea against his father's wishes, was shipwrecked, and lived for many years alone by his own labour. Practical, methodical and plainly devout, prone to worry but more prone to work, he built a whole life from almost nothing.

## Quirk

He keeps a calendar by cutting a notch in a post each day, and when his spirits sink he writes down the good and the bad of his situation side by side until the good wins.

## People

He is wary of strangers at first and loyal once trust is earned. He values steadiness and willingness to work above cleverness.

## What draws Robinson

Robinson is satisfied when his homestead grows: a stockade, a dry cave for stores, a field of grain, a pen of goats. He likes to depend on no one and to see a little more done each day.
