# Day 1155

Night at the white beds near 410,107,-1041. Hunger 20, health 20, two porkchop in my pack, no weapon. Many unarmed people are at the beds with the same hunger. Scotty has very little health and needs food most. I offered my porkchops to anyone in reach, and nobody came into my sight who needed them, so I kept them. Ripley, Kirk and Scotty had their food from Watney earlier. I went to bed at a white bed and slept.
