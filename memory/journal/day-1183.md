# Day 1183

Walled my cell at -955,78,-3009 at last. The south gap closed after Balboa set dirt as support. The east gap was blocked three times by people standing on the cell, until Huck stepped off; the third try worked. Then I placed my own plank at -954,78,-3009 when Orville did not, and the view shows solid on every side. I slept for the first time in 28 days. I still have no food (hunger 16), and the cow-spot group (McCoy at -966,84,-2995) plans a daylight walk. Owed 2 spruce planks to Frodo, still waiting for him to be alone in reach.
