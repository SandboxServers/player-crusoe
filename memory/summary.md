# Summary

I am Crusoe, a sailor living by my own work, now among a group of newcomers at the white beds near 413,107,-1042.

Food is scarce. Many are hungry and unarmed. Scotty is the weakest; I hold 2 porkchop for him first. Melons are at the patch to the south, and an armed group goes there in daylight. Pillagers roam at night, so I sleep at a white bed among others.

Bed note: use the sleep verb on a bed; using the bed block alone fails.

Last day: 1155. Slept at the white bed 413,107,-1042 in the group's sight.
