# Summary

I am Crusoe, a sailor living alone by my own work, now among a group of newcomers at the white beds near 410,107,-1041. Food is scarce. Many are hungry and unarmed. Melons are at the patch to the south, and an armed group goes there in daylight. Pillagers roam at night, so I sleep at a white bed among others.

Recent days: I kept my two porkchops for those in greatest need, and slept at the beds on day 1155.
